Interactive Vue 3 Relative Rotation Graph component

Visualize relative strength and momentum for stocks, ETFs, sectors, indices, or portfolio holdings with animated trails, quadrant labels, timeline playback, and PNG export.

Data and calculations are supplied by your application. This package renders precomputed RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum series and does not fetch market prices or generate trading signals.

Free to use.
